# Checks

- **The columns read as glass.** Each strip smears the colour beneath it, with clear seams between strips. Flat grey bars mean the group isn't pass-through, or the art isn't below the glass.
- **Softness.** `sharpness` under the glass should drop sharply, by about 60% or more, while areas outside the glass are unchanged. Compare with `hide: ["fractal-glass"]`.
- **The ridge isn't muddy.** If the image looks dirty, lower the strips' `fillOpacity`.
- **Faces and products** under strong blur look like a mistake. Keep them out of the glass area, or ask.
