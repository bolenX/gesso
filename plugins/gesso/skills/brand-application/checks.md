# Checks

- **Every role is on brand.** Headlines and body text use the brand font (`canvas_get` `@headline`, `@body`). Backgrounds, buttons and decorations use the brand hex values.
- **The logo is untouched:** original asset, aspect ratio kept, clear space respected. Check its frame against nearby layers.
- **Contrast.** Sample the CTA and its background: the luminance difference should be at least 50 levels. The text on the CTA is legible.
- **Every size.** Apply the brand once with roles, and every artboard follows. Spot-check each size.
