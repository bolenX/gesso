# Checks

- **Preflight on every artboard.** No errors, and no safe-zone warnings on story, reel and TikTok sizes.
- **Consistent.** Every size has the same headline text, CTA and logo. Compare with `canvas_get` across the artboards.
- **Nothing squeezed.** Subjects aren't cut by the edges. Render all artboards at 0.25 and look at them together.
- **Exports.** One file per artboard, named by channel and size, in the format each channel needs.
