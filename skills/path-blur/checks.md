# Checks

- **Direction.** Streaks follow the path in the direction of travel. Render the blurred area at 100%.
- **The subject stays sharp.** `sharpness` on the subject's region stays within about 10% of the original, and the background's drops.
- **The mask's edge doesn't show.** No visible halo or ring where the sharp subject meets the blur. Widen the mask's feather if there is.
