# Checks

- **Contrast survives.** `luminance.contrast` stays above about 70% of the original. If it drops further, darken the dark ink or add an S-curve below.
- **Exactly two inks.** Sample the darkest and lightest areas: they should read as the dark and light inks (within about ΔE 5). Mid-tones should fall on the line between them.
- **Faces stay readable.** Skin doesn't turn to a flat block. Check a face region at 100%.
- **Type contrast.** Type over the image has enough contrast against the duotone behind it.
