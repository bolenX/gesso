# Checks

- **The texture is inside the subject.** The face region changes clearly (ΔE well over 10), and the background outside the silhouette barely changes (ΔE under about 8).
- **Key features read.** The eyes or the face's outline are still recognisable in a small render.
- **One picture, not two.** The two images share a tone after the grade. Their average colours (`color.lab` per region) are close.
